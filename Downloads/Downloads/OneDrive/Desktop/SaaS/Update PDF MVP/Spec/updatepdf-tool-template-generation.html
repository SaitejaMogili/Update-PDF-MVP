FAQ

Common questions.

Is it really free?
Yes. The Invoice Generator template is free forever, with no watermark. You get 100 free credits per month, and each invoice costs 1 credit. So 100 free invoices per month. AI assist costs 1 extra credit. Pro lifts the cap and unlocks recurring invoices.
Does it support GST for India?
Yes. Switch the template region to India. You'll get GSTIN field, HSN/SAC code field, and CGST/SGST/IGST tax splits. The output is filing-ready.
Can I save recurring invoices?
On Pro plan. Save any invoice as recurring (weekly/monthly/quarterly). UpdatePDF generates the next invoice with auto-incremented number and updated dates, then emails it.
What's a brand kit?
Your saved logo, primary color, secondary color, and font family. Apply with one click to any of 100+ templates. Free users get one brand kit; Pro gets unlimited; Business gets team-wide locked brand kits.
How is this different from Canva or Word?
Canva is design-first; Word is layout-first. UpdatePDF is data-first. Type the numbers, get a PDF with the math right (subtotals, tax calc, totals). And the AI assist is genuinely good at industry-specific suggestions, not generic placeholder text.
